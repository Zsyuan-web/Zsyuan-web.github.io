# Needed Articles — Concepts Without Coverage

These concepts exist in the knowledge base but have no corresponding articles yet. Priority: High → Medium → Low.

## High Priority (Core Project Concepts)

### 空 / Śūnyatā
- **Why needed**: Central concept of entire project. Every article should reference it.
- **Approach**: Scene-driven introduction to emptiness. Not "what is śūnyatā" but "here's what happens when you stop trying to find permanent meaning."
- **Framework step**: 夺境 (Seizing Context)
- **Locale**: en first, zh second

### 道 / Dao
- **Why needed**: Core Taoist concept, primary target for "what is Taoism" searches
- **Approach**: Scene: "You've tried to figure out what life is about. Taoism says: stop trying to figure it out."
- **Framework step**: 用工具 (Using Tools)
- **Locale**: en first

### 无为 / Wú-wéi
- **Why needed**: Core Taoist practice. Pairs with Dao. Also practical for "how to stop trying so hard" searches.
- **Approach**: Scene: "You push harder. It doesn't work. What if the answer is to push less?"
- **Framework step**: 用工具 (Using Tools)
- **Locale**: en first

### 无我 / Anātman
- **Why needed**: Core Buddhist concept, directly addresses "who am I" existential search
- **Approach**: Scene: "You keep trying to be a better version of yourself. But what if there's no 'self' to improve?"
- **Framework step**: 夺境 (Seizing Context)
- **Locale**: en first

## Medium Priority

### 空空 / Double Emptiness
- **Why needed**: Step 3 of framework. Essential for readers who went through steps 1-2 and need the final move.
- **Approach**: "You've tried to accept emptiness. Now what?空空 says: even 'acceptance' is another thing to let go."
- **Framework step**: 空空 (Double Emptiness)
- **Locale**: en first

### 尼采 / Nietzsche
- **Why needed**: Primary "God is dead" mapping. Key for Western readers approaching from existential crisis.
- **Approach**: Scene: "凌晨三点，你想到'这一切有什么意义'。尼采说：是的，没有。你怎么活？"
- **Locale**: en first

### 西谷启治 / Nishitani
- **Why needed**: The bridge between Western nihilism and Buddhist emptiness. Core to project's unique positioning.
- **Approach**: "你穿过虚无主义了吗？西谷启治说：唯一的出路是穿过去，不是绕过去。"
- **Locale**: en first

### 存在虚无主义 / Existential Nihilism
- **Why needed**: Core problem statement. Answers "why does nothing matter?"
- **Approach**: Scene: "成功是空的，关系是短的，死亡让一切归零。这种感觉不是你的错——这是存在的症状。"
- **Locale**: en first
