# Nishitani Keiji (西谷启治)

## Core Thesis
Nihilism cannot be overcome by avoiding it — the only way out is through. The self must pass through the Great Doubt (a thoroughgoing existential crisis where one's very being becomes a question) into the Great Death (the dissolution of the ego-centered self), and emerge into the Field of Emptiness (śūnyatā).

Crucially, śūnyatā is not mere nothingness — it is the emptiness that empties even itself, the "nothingness of being" where all things exist in their true suchness (tathatā 如).

## 核心命题（中文）
虚无主义不能绕过去——唯一的出路是穿过去。自我必须经历大疑（连自己的存在本身都变成问号的彻底危机），进入大死（小我的解体），最终抵达空的场域（śūnyatā）。

## Relation to Buddhism
Nishitani is the key bridge figure. He uses Mahayana Buddhism (specifically the concept of śūnyatā) to directly answer Nietzsche's problem of nihilism. His framework — Great Doubt → Great Death → Field of Emptiness — is essentially a philosophical articulation of Zen practice.

## Buddhist Response (to his own framework)
空空——连空本身也空掉。西谷启治的"空的场域"如果被当成一个"境界"抓住，它就变成了新的执着。真正的解脱是连"解脱"这个概念也空掉。

## Taoist Response
无无——否定之否定。《庄子·齐物论》："有有也者，有无也者，有未始有无也者"。道家在"无"之后还看到了"无无"，再之后还有"未始有无"。

## Key Insight for This Project
西谷启治是整个项目的理论核心。他的框架（夺境→用工具→空空）直接对应知识图谱的三阶框架。他是京都学派对尼采的回答——虚无主义不是终点，是门。
