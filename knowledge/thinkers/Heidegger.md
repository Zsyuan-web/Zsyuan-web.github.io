# Martin Heidegger (海德格尔)

## Core Thesis
Nihilism is not a worldview people choose — it is the historical event of the forgetting of Being (Seinsvergessenheit). Because Being is not a being, from the perspective of beings it shows up as nothing. Western metaphysics has always asked about beings (what exists) while forgetting the question of Being itself (why existence at all?).

> "The nothing noths." — What Is Metaphysics?

## 核心命题（中文）
虚无主义不是人们选择的一种世界观——它是"存在被遗忘"这个历史事件。因为存在本身不是任何一个存在者，所以从存在者的视角看，它表现为无。

## Relation to Buddhism
Heidegger showed genuine interest in Daoism (he partially translated the Daodejing) and Zen. His idea that "the nothing noths" (Das Nichts selbst nichtet) echoes the Buddhist insight that emptiness is not a thing but an activity — the dynamic "emptying" of all fixed essences.

## Relation to Taoism
Mapped to 为学日益，为道日损 — learning adds, the Dao subtracts.

## Buddhist Response
海德格尔的"存在的遗忘"就是佛教的"无明"（avidyā）——对实相的根本无知。不知道一切法无自性，迷失在存在者（phenomena）中而遗忘存在本身（the nature of reality）。佛教的处方：先看到"遗忘"本身，再看到"有一个遗忘者"也是空的。

## Taoist Response
为学日益，为道日损。海德格尔花了一辈子追问"什么是存在"，老子说"道可道，非常道"——存在不能被定义为任何存在者。你越追问它，它越退隐。这不是失败，这是道的本质。

## Key Insight for This Project
海德格尔和佛教都看到了同一个问题：人迷失在"是什么"（beings）中，遗忘了"为什么有"（Being）。但佛教更进一步——连这个"遗忘者"也是空的，不需要被找到。
